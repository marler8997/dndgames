# Ruby Phoenix Tournament — Rules Notes

Our working notes on the qualifier rules on Bonmu. Compiled from the table read-through on
**May 17, 2026** and the recap with Ingdani on **Aug 16, 2026**. Where the two disagree, the
Aug 16 version wins — those are flagged **(corrected Aug 16)**.

## Where we stand

- We hold **6 silver feathers** on the necklace — 3 from the temple base, 2 from the Jai Bon
  enforcer payout, 1 Kyuu pulled out of a tree on the south coast. **None of them were won by
  fighting**, which matters for the cap below.
- Our application has been accepted. **The tournament starts the morning after the Aug 16 session.**
- Qualifier runs **3 days**. The first **8 teams** to present **10 feathers** at Mount Hamanabi
  advance.

## Issuing and accepting challenges

- Feathers are earned by challenging other teams to official matches. Any team can challenge any
  other once the qualifying round begins.
- A challenge must be declared **in the presence of an enforcer**. The challenged team must accept
  or be disqualified.
- **Safe hours:** no challenges between midnight and sunrise.
- The enforcer takes both teams to the nearest designated fighting site — official challenges
  happen in arenas.
- Two teams can also agree to fight **without a wager**. Then it can happen anywhere on the island,
  and the defending team is not required to accept.
- We have an **idol that lets us decline a match** — hand it to an enforcer. We can use it *after*
  accepting a challenge, and the declined team can't challenge us again for **12 hours**. (Society
  valued it around 500g; currently in the sell pile, maybe reconsider that.)
- After a match, the **winner cannot re-challenge the team it beat** for the rest of the event. The
  **loser can** demand rematches until they run out of feathers or stop wanting to.

## Wagers, feathers, and payouts

- The challenger wagers **1–3 feathers**; the defender must match the wager. Feathers are handed to
  the enforcer to hold.
- The winner takes all the wagered feathers **plus 500 gp**. **(corrected Aug 16 — we didn't have
  the gold payout written down.)**
- Enforcers may add to the winnings for a particularly spectacular or memorable match.
- **Looting the losing team is only permitted if its members were killed**, not merely knocked
  unconscious.

## The 10-feather cap

- **The cap only applies to feathers won by fighting. (corrected Aug 16.)** If a wager transfer
  would put a team over 10 total, the excess goes to the enforcers and is taken out of play for the
  qualifying round.
- Feathers **found in the wild or gifted** (the ones from the temple base, Gart's, the one Kyuu
  pulled out of a tree) are not clawed back, and the rule is **not retroactive**.
- A team at 10+ **cannot issue challenges** — but can still be challenged, still take enforcer
  challenges, and still find feathers. So there's no reason to stall below 10.

## Inside a match

- **Only the two teams may participate.** Neither team may accept aid from anyone not in the
  challenge.
   - An **animal companion counts as part of the team**, so Snapper is legal in an official match.
     (He sat out the Aug 16 friendly because Tino asked, and rode it out in the wand of pampered
     pet.)
- Free-for-all between both teams. **Last team standing wins.**
- A team may **yield and forfeit at any time**. Failing to honor a forfeit is a disqualification.
- **Killing inside a challenge is legal.** Harming an entrant *outside* a challenge disqualifies
  your team — no knifing people in their sleep.
- **No ties.** If a winner isn't obvious the enforcer has final say.

## Enforcers

- Recruited by the church to keep order on Bonmu and act as judges. They can oversee challenges and
  directly interfere with any team that breaks the rules.
- They also operate **as a team of their own**:
   - They are **not required to wager** feathers, and can require the defender to wager **up to 3**.
   - Beating them pays **500 gp instead of feathers**.
   - **No lethal action may be taken against an enforcer.**
- They can declare **unique non-combat challenges** — exploring the island, overcoming the
  environment — and hold extra rewards for them. That's where a couple of our feathers came from,
  and it's the way to keep earning once we're at 10.

## The Phoenix Necklace

Two separate activations:

1. **Non-lethal ward — 3 actions, command.** **(corrected Aug 16: this is 3 actions, not 10
   minutes.)** Select any number of willing creatures within 30 ft, including yourself. Those
   creatures **can make non-lethal attacks without taking the usual penalty to attack rolls**. It
   does **not** auto-convert damage. Lasts until you remove the necklace or activate it again.
2. **Raise Dead — 10 minutes**, consumes attached feathers. Casts **7th-rank Raise Dead** on one of
   us. **Each activation consumes one more feather than the previous one** (1, then 2, then 3...) —
   **(corrected Aug 16; we had it written as a flat one feather).** Three revives would cost 6
   feathers total.

Table rules:
- Call out **lethal vs non-lethal on every attack** during a match, not just once at the start.
- Negotiate before a challenge whether both teams are activating necklaces and going non-lethal.
  The wording only covers attack-roll penalties, so agree explicitly rather than assuming.
- Tino's team carries one too — Yaben wears theirs, with no feathers attached.
